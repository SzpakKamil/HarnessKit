The shadow opacity from 0 (fully transparent) to 1 (fully opaque).

## Overview

Controls how visible the shape shadow is against the background. Contact shadows typically use lower opacity values (around 0.2 to 0.4) to look natural, while artistic effects may use higher values.

The default value is `0.3`.
