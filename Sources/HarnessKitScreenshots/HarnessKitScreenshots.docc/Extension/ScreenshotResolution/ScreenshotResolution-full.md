A high-resolution preset at 2089 by 1440 points.

## Overview

Use `full` when you need the highest built-in resolution for rendering screenshots. The corresponding ``HarnessKitScreenshots/ScreenshotResolution/size`` returns a `CGSize` of 2089 by 1440. This is suitable for final export or high-fidelity preview contexts.
